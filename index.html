04 · About

A bit about me

I'm a backend engineer who cares about the parts of a product users never see but always feel: correct balances, permissions that make sense, and endpoints that stay fast as data grows. I like keeping views thin, putting business logic in services, and composing querysets deliberately so performance doesn't depend on luck.

Alongside engineering, I mentor and teach aspiring developers. Explaining systems clearly has made me better at designing them and at working with product, frontend and mobile teammates.
